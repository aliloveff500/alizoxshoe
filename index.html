Our Story

Turning Passion Into a Growing Brand

Welcome to ALIZOX — a brand built on passion, ambition, and a vision for style.

My name is Ali Hamid, and I am a 15-year-old student currently studying in Class 10th. I started ALIZOX with a dream of building something of my own and turning my passion for sneakers and fashion into a growing brand.

At ALIZOX, our goal is to make discovering stylish sneakers simple, enjoyable, and convenient. We believe that great style is not just about what you wear — it's about expressing your personality and confidence.
